Project overview

NeuroScholar AI is an AI-powered research platform that helps users explore multiple scientific papers through conversational research, literature-review workflows, paper comparison, trend analysis, and academic report generation. It combines semantic and keyword retrieval with agentic workflows so responses can be grounded in uploaded documents.

Research chat across PDFs

Upload multiple research papers and ask questions about their content.

Hybrid RAG retrieval

Combines FAISS semantic similarity with BM25 keyword matching and multi-query retrieval.

Research workflows

Supports literature reviews, paper comparisons, trend analysis, and structured reports.

Sessions and export

Persists research sessions using SQLite and supports downloadable PDF reports.

Agentic research workflow

  • PDF ingestion: parse uploaded PDFs, split text into chunks, and prepare content for retrieval.
  • Hybrid retrieval: use FAISS for semantic search and BM25 for keyword matching; multi-query retrieval broadens document discovery.
  • Planner agent: determines the task and the appropriate research workflow.
  • Retrieval and research agents: fetch relevant passages and develop document-grounded insights.
  • Analysis and citation components: support cross-document comparison and source references where available.
  • Report agent: supports structured academic output and PDF export.
  • Persistence: session and chat-history data are stored using SQLite and SQLAlchemy.

Features

  • Multi-PDF upload and document-grounded Q&A
  • Structured literature-review generation
  • Comparison of findings, methods, and limitations
